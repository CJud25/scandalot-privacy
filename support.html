Support — Scandalot

Last updated: 2026-08-28

Scandalot is a political-satire roguelite: you create a candidate, run for city council, and try to climb to the presidency without the scandals catching you first. This page is how you reach a human about it.

Contact

Email cjudkins24@gmail.com. It is a small independent project, so replies come from one person rather than a support desk — expect a couple of days rather than a couple of hours.

When reporting a problem, the two things that help most are your device model and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot is worth more than either.

Known issues

  • Some music is placeholder. Several game states currently reuse another track rather than their intended one, so the debate, victory and defeat music are not final. Sound effects are complete.
  • No cloud saves. Progress is stored on the device only. Uninstalling the app, or moving to a new phone, does not carry your legacy over.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection? No. Scandalot is fully offline and never connects to a network. There is no account and no sign-in.

Are there ads or in-app purchases? No, neither.

Where is my save data? In the app's private storage on your device. It is not readable by other apps and is removed when you uninstall.

How do I reset my progress? Uninstalling and reinstalling clears everything, including unlocked achievements and honorifics.

Are the parties and politicians real? No. Every party, office, official, newspaper and town in the game is invented. No real person, party, or country appears, and the satire is not aimed at any real-world platform.

The text or buttons are hard to read or move too fast. The settings screen (the gear on the candidate-creation screen) has a reduced-motion toggle along with separate music, sound-effect and haptics controls.
