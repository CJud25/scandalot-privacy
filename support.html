Support — Scandalot

Last updated: 2026-08-30

Scandalot is a political-satire roguelite: you create a candidate, run for city council, and try to climb to the presidency without the scandals catching you first. This page is how you reach a human about it.

Contact

Email cjudkins24@gmail.com. It is a small independent project, so replies come from one person rather than a support desk — expect a couple of days rather than a couple of hours.

When reporting a problem, the two things that help most are your device model and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot is worth more than either.

Save portability

Scandalot has no account-based cloud save or cross-platform sync. Its local data may be included in an operating-system device or cloud backup, depending on the backup settings and restore behavior of your platform.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection? No. Scandalot itself is fully playable offline and has no account or sign-in. If you choose Share the Front Page, the destination you select from the system share sheet may use its own network connection.

Are there ads or in-app purchases? No, neither.

Where is my save data? In the app's private storage on your device. The operating system may include it in a backup, depending on your settings.

How do I reset my progress? Uninstalling clears Scandalot's local copy, including unlocked achievements and honorifics. Restoring a platform backup may restore previously backed-up data.

Are the parties and politicians real? No real politicians or parties are portrayed. Candidates, parties, events, countries, newspapers, and towns are fictional, although familiar public-office titles appear as part of the satire.

The text or buttons are hard to read or move too fast. The settings screen (the gear on the candidate-creation screen) has a reduced-motion toggle along with separate music, sound-effect and haptics controls.
