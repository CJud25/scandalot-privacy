Privacy Policy — Scandalot

Last updated: 2026-08-30

Scandalot and its developer do not collect personal data. The game has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated backend.

What we collect. Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, or usage statistics. We never see anything you do in the app.

What is stored on your device. The game saves your progress and your settings (music volume, sound-effects volume, haptics, reduced motion) to your device's private application storage. This includes the candidate name you type when creating a character. The operating system may include this local data in a device or cloud backup, depending on your backup settings. Uninstalling removes the app's local copy; a backup may remain under the platform provider's privacy and retention terms.

User-initiated sharing. If you tap Share the Front Page, Scandalot creates a result image and text that may include your candidate name, then opens the system share sheet. You choose whether to share, what destination receives it, and who receives it. Scandalot and its developer do not receive a copy. A destination app or service may process the content under its own privacy policy.

Network access. Scandalot makes no network connections and is fully playable offline. There is no server, and we operate no backend for this app. A destination you choose from the system share sheet may use its own network connection outside Scandalot.

Third-party services. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It is built with Flutter and uses the open-source audioplayers, path_provider, and share_plus packages for sound, local files, and the system share sheet. None collects or transmits data on our behalf in this app. The app store, backup provider, and any share destination have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. It is not directed at children specifically, and it contains no chat, public profiles, multiplayer, advertising, or purchases. Sharing is optional and uses the device's system share sheet.

Your rights. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on your device, subject to any backups controlled through your platform account.

Changes. If a future version of the app collects any data, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here with a new "last updated" date.
