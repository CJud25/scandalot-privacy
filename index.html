Privacy Policy — Scandalot

Last updated: 2026-08-28

Scandalot does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What we collect. Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, or usage statistics. We never see anything you do in the app.

What is stored on your device. The game saves your progress and your settings (music volume, sound-effects volume, haptics, reduced motion) to your device's private application storage. This includes the candidate name you type when creating a character. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Network access. The app does not request the internet permission and makes no network connections. It is fully playable offline. There is no server, and we operate no backend for this app.

Third-party services. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It is built with the Flutter framework (Google) and uses the open-source audioplayers package for sound playback; neither collects or transmits data on our behalf in this app. The app store you installed from has its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. It is not directed at children specifically, and it contains no social features, no chat, and no purchases.

Your rights. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on your device.

Changes. If a future version of the app collects any data, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here with a new "last updated" date.
